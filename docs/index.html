Proj is expected DraftKings points: your model blended with salary, refit weekly against real box scores (projection_calibration.py); Ceil is your model's 97th-percentile outcome. Ownership = softmax by position of z(projection) + z(value), fitted on a real DraftKings classic contest and refit per sport with fit_ownership.py as contest exports come in. Lineups come from a pool solved per slate, spread from pure projection to heavily ownership-penalised; hockey adds 4-3, 4-2 and 3-3 team stacks and a 4-stack of each of the ten strongest teams, plus same-line (L1/L2), full line + defenceman, and PP1 stacks. Lines and PP units are each team's deployment in its LAST game (NHL shift charts), not tonight's confirmed lines.
